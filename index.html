On foot: W A S D to walk, Shift to run, drag to look. Click anything within reach: hood, trunk, fuel flap, tyres, door handles, engine parts. Walk back to the driver's door and press F to drive.

Drag anywhere to look around, scroll to zoom. Everything that lights up under the pointer can be clicked: door handles, window switches, glovebox, sun visors, cup holders, armrest, vents, stalks, knobs, seats and the touchscreen. Scroll over a knob to turn it.
